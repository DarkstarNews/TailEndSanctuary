# How to change the Tail End Sanctuary website

*(Print this page and keep it near your computer or iPad.)*

You can change the website's photos and words yourself — no computer skills
needed. You cannot break anything. Every change can be undone, and Ryan can
always put things back the way they were.

## Getting in

1. Open **tailendsanctuary.org/editor** in your web browser
   (Safari or Chrome, on your iPad or computer).
2. Type the password: **________________** *(Ryan will fill this in)*
3. Tap your name.

That's it — you're in the editing room.

## Making a change

The menu asks: **What would you like to do?**

- **📷 Change a photo** — pick the photo, tap "Choose a new photo", pick one
  from your photos, then press the big green button.
- **✏️ Change some words** — pick the page, tap the words you want to change,
  type the new words, then press the big green button.
- **🐕 The dogs** — tap a dog to change their photo or story, or tap
  "Add a new dog" when a new resident arrives.
- **💚 The founders** — your own photos and bios on the About page.
- **↩️ Undo my last change** — if you change your mind, this puts the most
  recent change back the way it was.

## Good to know

- After you press the green button, the website updates **within a minute or
  two**. If you're looking at the website, refresh the page to see it.
- Photos straight from your iPhone or iPad camera roll work perfectly — big
  photos are shrunk automatically.
- If something looks odd or you're not sure what to do, just stop and email
  Ryan. Nothing you do here can break the website.

## What still goes through Ryan

Bigger changes — adding a whole new page, changing the donation buttons or
bank details, changing how the site looks — still go to Ryan, same as before.
Email him what you'd like.
